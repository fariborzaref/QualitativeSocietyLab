## Purpose

Weber called it *Verstehen*: the recognition that social action cannot be explained without being understood from within. A regression can establish that welfare recipients distrust institutions; only sustained interpretive work can establish what that distrust means, how it is narrated, and why it persists. The qualitative tradition is not the soft counterpart of measurement. It is the discipline of making meaning-claims answerable to evidence.

This repository holds two connected bodies of work. The first is the interpretive apparatus behind my research on inequality, welfare institutions, and organizational discourse: interview protocols, focus group frameworks, and discourse analysis procedures. The second is the architecture of integration, the designs by which interpretive evidence and statistical evidence are made to bear on one another rather than sitting in adjacent chapters. Du Bois's *Philadelphia Negro* joined a survey of five thousand households to ethnographic observation before "mixed methods" was a term; the integration was the method, and it still is.

This repository pairs with [QuantitativeSocietyLab](https://github.com/fariborzaref/QuantitativeSocietyLab), which holds the statistical models, and [ComputationalSociologyHub](https://github.com/fariborzaref/ComputationalSociologyHub), which holds the methods of scale. Materials are released in stages as the underlying studies conclude.

## A Note on What Is Shared

No participant data appears in this repository. Interview transcripts, focus group recordings, and derived analysis files contain confidential material governed by consent agreements and institutional review, and they are not suitable for public archiving. What is shared is the methodological apparatus: protocols, guides, coding frameworks, codebook templates, integration designs, and worked exemplars constructed from synthetic or public-domain text. This is the material replication actually requires, and it can be shared without compromising the people who made the research possible.

## The Interpretive Program

**In-Depth Interviews.** Semi-structured protocols and thematic analysis frameworks for studying how individuals narrate class position, institutional encounters, and economic insecurity. The documentation supports both inductive coding and theory-driven deductive schemes.

**Focus Groups.** Guides and analytic matrices for collective settings, where the object of study is not the individual account but the interaction: consensus formation, structured dissent, and the small politics of who speaks for whom.

**Institutional Discourse Analysis.** Stepwise frameworks for analyzing the texts institutions produce about themselves: policy documents, reports, mission statements. This module interoperates with the corpus-scale text methods of ComputationalSociologyHub, so that close reading and computation can discipline one another.

## The Integration Program

**Convergent and Sequential Designs.** Templates for parallel-strand studies merged at interpretation, and for sequences in which statistical findings define what fieldwork must explain, or fieldwork generates the constructs that measurement then tests. Divergence between strands is treated as a finding to be adjudicated, not a flaw to be suppressed.

**Joint Displays and Meta-Inference.** The integration of evidence must be visible, not asserted. Joint display templates place statistical and interpretive findings in a single analytic frame, and the meta-inference documentation specifies how conclusions from combined evidence are warranted and bounded.

**Case Selection at the Interface.** Which cases deserve close study is itself an inferential decision. Frameworks here use model results, residuals, deviant cases, and influential observations, to direct qualitative inquiry where the models are least adequate.

## Planned Architecture

Modules are added as the corresponding studies conclude:

```
/Interview_Protocols      Semi-structured guides and consent-safe documentation templates
/Focus_Group_Frameworks   Moderation guides, coding matrices, interaction analysis notes
/Discourse_Analysis       Stepwise procedures and exemplar analyses of institutional texts
/Integration_Designs      Convergent and sequential design templates, joint displays
/Codebooks                Template codebooks and coding reliability documentation
```
